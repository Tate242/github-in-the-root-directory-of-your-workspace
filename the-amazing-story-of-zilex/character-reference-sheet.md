## The World

- **Planet:** Nopaltia — a huge alien civilization split into 7 kingdoms
- **Kingdoms & rulers:** Zorphia (King Zor), Alroxia (King Alroy), Toplic (King Taph), Zarad (King Dopal), Dorox (King Xorol), Xalia (Queen Alia — prodigy, first female monarch), Baldolph (King Doba)
- **Zilex's home:** the Kingdom of Xalia — Queen Alia is *his* ruler; commoners kneel to her
- **The Ceremony of Kings:** the public ceremony where commoners kneel to the rulers; site of the mass stand-up and violent guard crackdown on the night the star fell
- **Power stones:** each ruler holds one — symbol of absolute power, bestows unmatched power on its owner
- **The fallen star:** a silver-platinum stone that fell from the sky and bonded exclusively with Zilex. It actively called him by name before bonding; it burns anyone else who touches it. Visual signature: it glows with veins of light. It bonds with Zilex during Part 1 — after bonding he can hold it bare-handed
- **The cave:** the fallen star landed in a crater inside a cave in rocky hills; the cave is now Zilex and May's refuge and base of operations
- **Remote sensing:** Queen Alia feels the fallen star's bonding the instant it happens, from any distance — her own stone flickers when it awakens. She does NOT know the bonder's identity, name, or location
- **Stone hierarchy:** ruler stones can flinch — even "fear" — when a greater power awakens; the fallen star ranks above all seven ruler stones. Alia's stone had "never blinked" before it, and the flinch persists into the day after the bonding
- **The crackdown:** after the ceremony stand-up, Alia had all involved enslaved (~a thousand rebels); the hunt for survivors began at dawn the next day. Alia interrogates imprisoned rebels about the fallen object
- **Witnessed descent:** the star's fall was seen publicly — ceremony rebels "saw something fall that night"; Alia has deduced an object fell
