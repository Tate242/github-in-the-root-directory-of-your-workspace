## Director's Notes

- **Villain POV switch** — Parts 1–2 were Zilex's voice; handing Part 3 to Alia is the retention spike and humanizes the antagonist before the collision.
- **"Little flame"** — Alia senses a presence but knows no name or location; the line is a threat, not sight. The cave-mouth cutaway is audience dramatic irony. The name reveal is saved for their first face-to-face.
- **"My stone fears it"** — establishes that ruler stones can *fear*, raising the fallen star above all seven in the power hierarchy.
- **Production notes:** hold the hook's ellipsis pause over the stone-flicker visual, not between words (~3.2s read — no slack). In the 40–45s frame, keep the dark choker stone in shot so "It chose wrong" has its visual anchor.
- Part 4 returns to Zilex's POV: the hunt closes in on the cave, and the stone shows him its first real power.
